# Privacy

- **No cookies, no tracking, no personal data.** The site sends nothing about you anywhere.
- **Stored in your browser only** (`localStorage`):
  - that you've seen the intro;
  - that you've seen the Drive coach marks;
  - in developer mode, your theme choice.

  Clearing site data removes all of it.

- **Sound** is off until you switch it on.
- If anonymous usage analytics are ever added, they will be off by default and described here first.

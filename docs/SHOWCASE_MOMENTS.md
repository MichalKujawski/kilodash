# Showcase moments

One entry per finished feature, for building a guided demo. Keep each entry short.

## Template

### Feature name

- **Demo:** what happens in the world, and what appears on the cluster.
- **Caption:** one line for visitors.
- **Duration:** ~N s

## Entries

### Guided demo: Motorway

- **Demo:** rolling onto the A 7. Adaptive cruise switches on and follows the car ahead. The view changes
  to driver assistance with green lane lines. A fast car comes up on the left as we indicate, and the
  blind-spot warning flashes. We wait, then move over.
- **Caption:** "Adaptive cruise keeps the speed and the distance by itself."
- **Duration:** ~30 s

### Guided demo: Emergency braking

- **Demo:** the driver looks away. Traffic ahead brakes hard. Pre-warning, then a red warning with a brake
  jolt, then automatic braking; the driver looks up and takes over.
- **Caption:** "Traffic ahead brakes hard. Warning, brake jolt, then automatic braking."
- **Duration:** ~15 s

### Take the wheel (Drive dock)

- **Demo:** the visitor clicks Take the wheel on the motorway. The cruise buttons on the right spoke light
  up. Pressing the cruise button and SET shows the set speed in the cluster. Tapping the stalk blinks the
  green arrows in step with the cluster's lamps.
- **Caption:** "Your turn: the same buttons a real wheel has, lit where they work."
- **Duration:** ~15 s

### Engineer mode: behaviour you can see

- **Demo:** switch to Engineer during the Safety chapter. In the collision-warning diagram the state
  steps ready → prewarn → acute → braking, each transition flashing. The Events tab lists the same
  steps with speed and gap. "How it works" opens the feature doc with links to the source.
- **Caption:** "Every warning is a small, tested state machine. Here they are, live."
- **Duration:** ~20 s
